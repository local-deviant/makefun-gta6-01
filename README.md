ISLA FORTUNA — a 3D open-world crime game on a sun-baked island port city. Dante "El Rayo" Cruz, a former street racer turned mechanic, has seven days to raise $500,000 and buy his younger brother Marco out of a cartel debt — a debt owed to Don Salvador "El Tiburón" Vega, the boss who owns the Fortuna Casino and runs the port. Drive and steal cars through 8 named districts, work 7 story missions with portrait dialogue and named characters, race 3 street circuits against rivals Vex, Rosa and Tomas, and stay ahead of a deep 1–5 star wanted system: a police helicopter you can shoot down, real roadblocks that stop you cold, SWAT at five stars, witnesses who phone the police after 2.5 seconds, and car swaps plus garage resprays to shake the heat. On the side: taxi fares, 15 hidden Sun Discs, a weapon shop, a garage, respect ranks and a % COMPLETE tracker, 4 radio stations, photo mode and a day/night cycle. Built with vendored three.js (WebGL), fully procedural visuals, no network, deterministic.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
python3 -m http.server --directory game 8080
# open http://localhost:8080
```
