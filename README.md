ISLA FORTUNA — a 3D open-world crime game. Dante "El Rayo" Cruz, a former street racer turned mechanic, must raise $500,000 to free his brother Marco from Don Salvador "El Tiburón" Vega, the cartel boss who owns the Fortuna Casino. Drive and steal cars across the island city of Isla Fortuna (8 named districts), run 7 story missions with named characters and portrait dialogue, race 3 street circuits against named rivals (Vex, Rosa, Tomas), and outrun the police through a deep 1–5 star wanted system — a helicopter that can be shot down (3 hits), real roadblocks, SWAT, witnesses who phone the police after 2.5 s, car swaps and resprays to clear heat. Side work: taxi fares, 15 hidden Sun Discs, weapon shop, garage, respect ranks, % COMPLETE. Dense HUD with mission tracker, police unit list, radio (4 stations), minimap + GPS, pause menu MAP/STATS/STORY/SETTINGS. Built with vendored three.js (WebGL), procedural visuals, no network, deterministic.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
python3 -m http.server --directory game 8080
# open http://localhost:8080
```
